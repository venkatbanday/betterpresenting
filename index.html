        FAQ

        Is it really free and private?

        Yes. Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded to a server. History is stored in your browser only. Live recording uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition, which some browsers send to their vendor’s speech service.

        Is this live coaching or an AI model?

        Audio and video are transcribed by an open-source AI speech model (Whisper) running locally. Scoring uses transparent rule-based metrics, not a human coach. Use it alongside human evaluators.

        My video is very large. What should I do?

        Extract the audio (MP3/M4A) first, or paste the YouTube auto-generated transcript with your speaking time.

        Why are delivery and manner self-rated?

        Gestures and eye contact can’t be heard in audio, so you rate them honestly. Manner blends your rating with confidence language.
