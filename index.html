        How it works

        1. Capture

        Record with your microphone (live transcription) or paste a transcript.

        2. Analyse

        Pace, fillers, hedging, structure, storytelling, vocabulary, sentence variety and timing are measured.

        3. Improve

        Get scores on eight contest criteria, prioritised steps and a coaching script. Re-evaluate and track progress.

        Who it’s for: Toastmasters speeches, contest preparation, keynotes, business presentations, conference talks and leadership communication.

        FAQ

        Is it really free and private?

        Yes. Everything runs in your browser. Your speech is never uploaded. Progress is stored in your browser’s local storage only. (Browser speech recognition may use your browser vendor’s speech service, depending on the browser.)

        Is this live coaching or a real AI?

        It is an automated analysis using transparent rule-based metrics, not a human coach or a large language model. Use it alongside human evaluators for the full picture.

        Can I upload a video or audio file?

        Browsers can’t transcribe files directly. Paste a transcript instead (YouTube auto-captions, Otter, Whisper and Word dictation all work) and enter your speaking time.

        Why are delivery and manner self-rated?

        Gestures and eye contact can’t be read from text, so those are your own honest ratings, blended with language cues for manner.
